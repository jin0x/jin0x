# The Hero's Journey — Interactive Terminal Narrative

## Overview

This document contains the complete narrative structure for the interactive terminal experience on John Leskas's GitHub profile. The experience is built around Campbell's Hero's Journey (monomyth) adapted for a developer/tech audience.

---

## The 12 Stages

### Stage 1: The Ordinary World (echo)
**Title:** The Starting Zone
**Subtitle:** The Ordinary World

> You awaken in a world of default states and rigid grids. Familiar, comfortable — yet hollow. Something tugs inside, like a breakpoint waiting to be hit.

**Lesson:** Comfort is not clarity. You were meant for more.
**Hint:** It echoes in every silent loop.
**Answer:** `echo`

---

### Stage 2: Call to Adventure (glitch)
**Title:** Quest Notification
**Subtitle:** Call to Adventure

> A flicker in the terminal. A glitch behind the UI. A message not meant for everyone. But it reached you.

**Lesson:** The code doesn't call everyone. But it called you.
**Hint:** What do you call an error that feels intentional?
**Answer:** `glitch`

---

### Stage 3: Refusal of the Call (doubt)
**Title:** Abort Mission?
**Subtitle:** Refusal of the Call

> You almost shut the tab. You almost listened to the voice that said 'not yet.' You almost believed comfort was enough.

**Lesson:** Every hero doubts before they begin.
**Hint:** The name of the shadow we all meet first.
**Answer:** `doubt`

---

### Stage 4: Meeting the Mentor (mirror)
**Title:** The Dev in the Shadows
**Subtitle:** The Mentor Appears

> Not a teacher. A mirror. Not an answer. A question. You realize: mentorship isn't found. It's remembered.

**Lesson:** Truth isn't given. It's remembered.
**Hint:** What reflects your potential back to you?
**Answer:** `mirror`

---

### Stage 5: Crossing the Threshold (trust)
**Title:** Crossing the Firewall
**Subtitle:** Crossing the Threshold

> No going back. You step beyond tutorials, beyond templates, into uncharted input. You become a player.

**Lesson:** Beyond the firewall lies your real self.
**Hint:** The only way forward is through what?
**Answer:** `trust`

---

### Stage 6: Tests, Allies, Enemies (resolve)
**Title:** The Debug Zone
**Subtitle:** Tests, Allies, Enemies

> False allies. True bugs. Edge cases that test more than your logic. You learn to read between lines, not just of code, but of people.

**Lesson:** The test is not survival. It's alignment.
**Hint:** What must hold when all else fails?
**Answer:** `resolve`

---

### Stage 7: The Inmost Cave (core)
**Title:** The Core Dump
**Subtitle:** Approach to the Inmost Cave

> You don't face dragons. You face an old version of yourself. You enter the repository of doubt.

**Lesson:** You face yourself, or you face nothing.
**Hint:** Go deeper. What's beneath every abstraction?
**Answer:** `core`

---

### Stage 8: The Ordeal (zero)
**Title:** System Crash
**Subtitle:** The Ordeal

> The project fails. The system crashes. You break. And in the break — a truth. You aren't the code. You are the spark.

**Lesson:** The death of who you were is the birth of who you could be.
**Hint:** What number precedes every build?
**Answer:** `zero`

---

### Stage 9: The Reward (source)
**Title:** The Source Code
**Subtitle:** Reward (Seizing the Sword)

> You regain control — not of the system, but of yourself. You see how everything can be rewritten. Including you.

**Lesson:** Clarity isn't given. It's claimed.
**Hint:** What powers everything, even you?
**Answer:** `source`

---

### Stage 10: The Road Back (signal)
**Title:** Return to the Starting Zone
**Subtitle:** The Road Back

> The codebase hasn't changed. But you have. You debug life differently now. Others start to follow your commits.

**Lesson:** Wisdom isn't what you say. It's what you sense.
**Hint:** What do systems send to find each other?
**Answer:** `signal`

---

### Stage 11: Resurrection (radiant)
**Title:** Patch 2.0
**Subtitle:** Resurrection

> Not rebirth. Re-integration. You bring all your versions back into one. You are no longer fragmented. You are forged.

**Lesson:** You are not the hero. You are the path.
**Hint:** What burns brighter after it breaks?
**Answer:** `radiant`

---

### Stage 12: Return with the Elixir (ignite)
**Title:** New Game+
**Subtitle:** Return with the Elixir

> You return, not to show off — but to show the way. You've become what others seek.

**Lesson:** Give it freely. Light the next mind.
**Hint:** What does every real flame do?
**Answer:** `ignite`

## Hidden Commands

### `whoami`
> "You are the result of every choice you didn't run away from." — John Leskas

### `chooseMentor()`
Reveals the mentor archetype selection.

### `showCodex()`
Opens the hidden lore codex.

### `loreFragment('EMBER')`
Reveals a hidden lore fragment about the origin.

### `traceSignal('origin/3.14')`
Easter egg that traces back to the beginning.

### `unlockOrigin()`
Unlocks the complete origin story.

### `meetTheCreator()`
Final easter egg — reveals John's personal message.

## Archetypes (Unlockable at Stage 12)

1. **The Architect** — System builders, pattern recognizers
2. **The Flamebearer** — Those who carry the spark to others
3. **The Oracle** — Intuition-led, pattern seers
4. **The Weaver** — Connection makers, bridge builders
5. **The Alchemist** — Transformers, pressure-to-gold converters

---

## Branching Paths (Stage 6)

At Stage 6, players can choose one of three paths:

1. **Logic** (`choose('logic')`) — The analytical path
2. **Intuition** (`choose('intuition')`) — The instinctive path
3. **Connection** (`choose('connection')`) — The collaborative path

All paths converge at Stage 7 (The Inmost Cave).
